This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Hoodcast app or visit related sites.

Information We Collect

Hoodcast does not collect or store any personally identifiable information from users.

No account creation, login, or personal data entry is required to use the app.

Our server may automatically log some basic technical details — such as your IP address, browser type, and access times — solely for security and operational purposes.

Apple may also share limited, non-identifying information with us through their developer tools. This may include crash logs, performance analytics, and general usage statistics. This information is aggregated and anonymous and does not include anything that can personally identify you.

How We Use Information

Any information we receive is used strictly to maintain, operate, and improve Hoodcast and its related services.

We may also use it to detect and prevent abuse, troubleshoot technical issues, and ensure the reliability of the app.

We may disclose information if required by law, such as in response to a subpoena, court order, or other legal process; to exercise our legal rights or defend against claims; or if necessary to investigate, prevent, or take action against unlawful activities, suspected fraud, or violations of our policies.

California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA)

We comply with CalOPPA. We do not share or sell personal information because we do not collect personal information in the first place. If that ever changes, we would only share information with your consent.

Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Hoodcast does not knowingly collect or store any information from children under the age of 13. The app and website are not designed to target or attract children.

European Union Users

By using Hoodcast or visiting its website, you acknowledge and consent to the processing of information (if any is collected through analytics or logging) outside of the European Union.

International Data Transfers

Any technical or anonymous information we receive may be processed or stored on servers located in the United States or other countries. Data protection laws in these locations may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

Your Consent
